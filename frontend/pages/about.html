About TrustLens Platform

Empowering Digital Citizens Through Explainable Document Intelligence

TrustLens AI was conceived as an enterprise-grade AI solution to combat misinformation, misleading official notices, and predatory digital documents through transparent, human-centered artificial intelligence.

PLATFORM OVERVIEW

The Core Problem & Platform Overview

Every day, millions of students, job seekers, and tenants receive digital circulars, offer letters, and rental agreements via messaging apps and social channels. Many of these documents contain altered numbers, predatory registration fee demands, or unverified links designed to deceive.

The TrustLens Solution

TrustLens AI acts as an intelligent visual filter — extracting structured data, calculating a transparent Trust Rating score, spotlighting side-by-side document inconsistencies, and explaining exactly why a document requires further official verification.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Modular Architecture & Hybrid Pipeline

TrustLens AI is engineered with a hybrid processing model combining local RegEx pattern matching, NLP text extraction (PDF, DOCX, TXT, OCR), ML spam classification, and Google Gemini 1.5 Pro integration. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

AI SAFETY & ETHICS

AI Safety, Privacy Shield & Ethical AI

Our Privacy Shield automatically strips and masks PII (Emails, Phones, IDs, Addresses) before cloud AI processing. TrustLens AI adheres to strict explainability guidelines — every risk score is accompanied by human-readable explanations and source cross-references without opaque black-box decisions.
